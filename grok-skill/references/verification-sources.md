# Recommended Public Verification Sources

## Taiwan

- Ministry of Economic Affairs company search (FindBiz / 商工登記查詢)
- Taiwan trademark search
- Local agent / distributor company records

## Mainland China

- National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System (GSXT / 国家企业信用信息公示系统)
- Commercial databases that aggregate GSXT + litigation + ownership graphs (企查查, 天眼查 style)

## Hong Kong

- Hong Kong Companies Registry
- Governing law and jurisdiction clauses in terms of service

## International

- USPTO trademark search (especially for brands claiming US origin)
- Official brand websites in different markets (Taiwan vs Sweden / US / global) for narrative comparison
- Stock exchange filings (Shenzhen, Hong Kong, etc.) for listed companies
- Privacy policies and data-transfer clauses for connected products
- Payment processor display names and registration jurisdiction for sponsorship / donation platforms

## Usage Guidance

- Prefer primary registry records over secondary news summaries.
- Always note the date of the information.
- When market-specific websites differ in founding story or headquarters, record both versions.
- Distinguish controlling ownership from minority investment.
